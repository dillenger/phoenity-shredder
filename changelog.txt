4.0
Content Security Policies in Thunderbird 153.2.0esr and Thunderbird 155.0 break the Phoenity Icons add-on.
This theme can be used as a replacement, these are the original icons as they are used in the Phoenity Icons add-on.
This theme, like Phoenity Icons, is loosely based on the original Phoenity Shredder theme, which can not be updated.
Unfortunately it can not be combined with other (background) themes, it will respect the OS theme (dark/light).
